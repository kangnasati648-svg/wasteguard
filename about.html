ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Building cleaner communities through technology.

EcoSphere combines digital reporting, data monitoring and community participation to create a smarter approach to waste management.

The Problem

Improper waste disposal creates unhealthy surroundings, blocked drainage systems, pollution and environmental damage. One major challenge is the lack of an easy way for citizens to report waste problems and track their resolution.

Our Solution

EcoSphere provides a centralized platform where users can report waste locations, categorize problems and monitor their status. The dashboard helps visualize waste-management data and identify areas requiring attention.
